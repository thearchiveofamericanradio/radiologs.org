## Summary

Superbly preserved digital archive of America's oldest municipal radio station (WNYC, est. 1924). Features direct 16-inch lacquer transfers of Fiorello LaGuardia's 'Talk to the People', WWII home-front dispatches, folk/jazz festivals, and early radio drama workshops, though scoped strictly to WNYC/NYC origin broadcasts.

## Provenance & Archival Methodology

- **Primary Master Source:** Original WNYC Municipal Broadcasting System 16" lacquer discs and ¼-inch master tapes
- **Air Date Methodology:** WNYC master broadcast logs, Master Control sheets, and *New York Times* / *Brooklyn Daily Eagle* radio listings
- **Cast Attribution Method:** Archivist-curated speaker, musician, and commentator records
- **Caption / Subtitle Engine:** Select curated transcripts (AAPB integration)

## Limitations, Trade-Offs & Gotchas

- **[NOTE] Scoped to WNYC / NYC Municipal Broadcasts Only:** Does not host commercial network entertainment series (*Jack Benny*, *Suspense*, *Lux Radio Theatre*) unless they intersected with WNYC.
