# UMKC Marr Sound Archives

Official URL: https://library.umkc.edu/marr  
Category: Institutional Sound Archive · Operator: University of Missouri–Kansas City Libraries · Founded: 1986  
Benchmark Card: https://www.radiologs.org/data/providers/umkc-marr.md · JSON: https://www.radiologs.org/data/providers/umkc-marr.json

## Summary

The physical institutional repository housing J. David Goldin's legendary 16-inch transcription disc collection and KMOX/CBS regional masters. Transfers audio at museum-grade 24-bit/96kHz resolution, but only ~7% of its radio holdings are playable remotely over the web.

## Pricing & Access Regime

- **Model:** institutional ($0 (Institutional))
- **Monthly / Annual USD:** $0/mo · $0/yr
- **Billing Note:** Public university special collection; select digital collections free online.
- **Free Tier / Access Note:** ~6,500 digitized recordings streamable via UMKC Digital Commons; 350,000+ physical items on campus.

## Provenance & Archival Methodology

- **Primary Master Source:** Physical home of the J. David Goldin 16" ET Collection, CBS/KMOX discs, and Raymond R. Wile Collection
- **Air Date Methodology:** Archival finding aids & physical 16" disc label metadata

- **Caption / Subtitle Engine:** None (Select oral histories only)
