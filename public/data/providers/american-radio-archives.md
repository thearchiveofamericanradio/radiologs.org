## Summary

Major Southern California special collection holding the personal archives of Carlton E. Morse (*I Love a Mystery*, *One Man's Family*), Norman Corwin, Milton Merlin, and KNX/CBS Hollywood. Extraordinary primary evidence for West Coast broadcasting, though only ~11% is digitized for online streaming.

## Pricing & Access Regime

- **Model:** institutional ($0 (Institutional))
- **Monthly / Annual USD:** $0/mo · $0/yr
- **Billing Note:** Public municipal/foundation special collection; online exhibits free, physical holdings require Thousand Oaks, CA visit.
- **Free Tier / Access Note:** ~3,200 digitized scripts, photos, and audio recordings online via Calisphere / library portal.

## Provenance & Archival Methodology

- **Primary Master Source:** Personal papers, scripts, and 16" acetates of Norman Corwin, Carlton E. Morse, Rudy Vallée, and KNX/CBS Pacific Network
- **Air Date Methodology:** Original creator production scripts, studio timing sheets, and KNX master logs
- **Cast Attribution Method:** Archival finding aids & handwritten cast sheets on original studio scripts
- **Caption / Subtitle Engine:** Scanned PDF studio scripts (non-synchronized)
