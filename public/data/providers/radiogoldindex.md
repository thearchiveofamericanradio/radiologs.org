## Summary

Compiled over 40+ years by former CBS/NBC engineer J. David Goldin from his personal collection of 95,000+ 16-inch transcription discs and tapes. One of the most authoritative discographical text references in radio history, **but hosts zero audio files** and uses a static 1990s frame-based HTML layout.

## Provenance & Archival Methodology

- **Primary Master Source:** Direct physical inspection of 95,000+ 16" ET discs and master tapes in the J. David Goldin Collection (now at UMKC Marr)
- **Air Date Methodology:** Transcribed directly from physical studio transcription disc labels, matrix etchings, and audition logs
- **Cast Attribution Method:** Exhaustive actor, announcer, director, writer, and musician credits verified by ear and disc label
- **Caption / Subtitle Engine:** None (Metadata index only)

## Limitations, Trade-Offs & Gotchas

- **[CRITICAL] Zero Audio Playback or Downloads (0% Streamable):** RadioGOLDINdex is strictly a discographical text encyclopedia. Users looking to actually listen to the broadcasts must cross-reference them on RadioIndex or visit UMKC Marr.
- **[WARNING] Static Alphabetical HTML Frames Without Query Engine:** Cannot run compound queries (e.g., 'all CBS broadcasts from October 1948 featuring William Conrad') without downloading and parsing thousands of static HTML files.
