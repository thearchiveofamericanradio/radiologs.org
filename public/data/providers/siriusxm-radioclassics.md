## Summary

Curated 24/7 linear satellite/app radio channel hosted by OTR historian Greg Bell, paired with rotating 2-hour on-demand themed blocks in the SiriusXM app. Offers superb verbal historical context and clean audio restoration, but provides no searchable archive, no permanent on-demand episode library, and DRM-locked streams.

## Limitations, Trade-Offs & Gotchas

- **[CRITICAL] Not an On-Demand Archive (~420 Rotating Shows vs. 100,000+):** You cannot look up and play a specific episode of *Suspense* or *Escape* on demand; you can only listen to the live linear stream or ~20 rotating 2-hour on-demand blocks.
- **[CRITICAL] Highest Annual Cost ($120–$280/yr) + DRM Lock-In:** Requires an ongoing SiriusXM subscription with notorious cancellation friction and zero offline file ownership.
